## 2. Product definition

VaakMitra is an offline-first, avatar-led Tamil **word-practice** companion for children aged 7–14 who have autism and are already supported by a therapist, special educator, or caregiver. It presents an approved Tamil word, listens to one attempt, estimates whether the attempted word matches the target, responds gently, chooses the next approved activity, and records minimum pseudonymous metrics.

The application is assistive educational technology. It is not a medical device, diagnostic system, autism detector, therapist replacement, or proof of clinical improvement.

### 2.1 Product goals

- **PG-01:** Keep a child engaged through predictable, short, natural avatar-led word sessions.
- **PG-02:** Assess known Tamil target words using child-robust, uncertainty-aware local inference.
- **PG-03:** Prefer neutral abstention over a confident but unsupported judgement.
- **PG-04:** Personalize the next safe activity without allowing learning algorithms to alter correctness.
- **PG-05:** Operate throughout the child session without internet access.
- **PG-06:** Prevent child audio and voice-derived representations from leaving the trusted edge device.
- **PG-07:** Give therapists auditable word-level trends and full control of the approved curriculum.
- **PG-08:** Support research claims only after child-separated, therapist-labelled validation.

### 2.2 Explicit non-goals

- **NG-01:** Phoneme, syllable, forced-alignment, GOP, VSA, or pronunciation-error localization in production V1.
- **NG-02:** ASD diagnosis, severity prediction, distress diagnosis, or emotion/stress inference from face or voice.
- **NG-03:** Unrestricted conversation, autonomous clinical advice, or LLM-authored child prompts.
- **NG-04:** Cloud ASR, cloud voice conversion, cloud scoring, or runtime model download during a session.
- **NG-05:** Generative child-to-adult voice conversion in the decision path.
- **NG-06:** Optimizing only time seated, compliance, or number of attempts.
- **NG-07:** Public target-population accuracy or clinical-efficacy claims before the release gates in Section 18 pass.

## 4. Research novelties

### NOV-01 — Safety-calibrated multi-view Tamil word assessment

The research contribution is a word-level assessment method for autistic-child Tamil speech that combines:

1. the original utterance;
2. deterministic vocal-tract/rate/loudness normalization;
3. Tamil IndicConformer token evidence;
4. a closed-vocabulary word classifier over encoder representations;
5. embedding-level DTW against approved target references;
6. calibrated uncertainty, cross-view disagreement, and an explicit abstention class.

The claim to evaluate is improved wrong-word safety and usable coverage relative to a raw adult-trained Tamil ASR baseline. The project must not claim to be the first without a documented literature review.

Required ablation rows are: raw model; raw plus normalized view; plus classifier; plus embedding DTW; full fusion; full fusion plus abstention. Report false PASS, false RETRY, UNSCORABLE coverage, calibration error, AUROC/AUPRC, latency, and subgroup results.

### NOV-02 — Clinician-constrained contextual-bandit personalization

The second contribution is an adaptive word-practice policy that learns which **approved support action** is most helpful for a child in a given context while deterministic rules retain authority over safety, curriculum, and correctness.

The initial learner is LinUCB. Contextual Thompson Sampling is the required comparator. Learning begins offline, then shadow mode, and only then a supervised online pilot. The policy optimizes helpful progress and voluntary engagement, not obedience or session length.

## 8. Audio contract and validation

- **AU-001:** Input is a bounded WAV, WebM/Opus, OGG/Opus, MP4/AAC, or PCM stream accepted only when a local decoder is installed. Unsupported codecs return HTTP 415 and `UNSCORABLE` semantics.
- **AU-002:** Decoded model input is float32 mono PCM, 16,000 Hz, finite values, range `[-1, 1]`.
- **AU-003:** Request limit is 8 MiB; decoded duration is 0.30–12.0 seconds; speech region is 0.20–8.0 seconds.
- **AU-004:** Reject empty input, undecodable input, NaN/Inf, excessive clipping, silence/no speech, speech below minimum duration, unsupported duration, and prompt leakage when reference playback evidence is available.
- **AU-005:** Default clipping limit is 1% of samples at absolute amplitude at least 0.999.
- **AU-006:** Quality includes RMS, peak, clipping ratio, speech duration, total duration, and boolean flags. These remain local.
- **AU-007:** VAD merges speech regions separated by up to 700 ms, configurable from 300–1200 ms, to avoid cutting within-word pauses.
- **AU-008:** The production VAD must actually execute its local model. An unloaded ONNX session may not silently fall back and claim production readiness.
- **AU-009:** Energy VAD is a labelled test/degraded mode only. It may determine `UNSCORABLE`; it cannot enable PASS in clinical mode.
- **AU-011:** Executable ONNX VAD configuration (model SHA-256, speech threshold, minimum speech duration, and pause merge tolerance) is versioned and hash-bound to calibration. Missing or mismatched bindings prevent production scoring; an engineering diagnostic may execute it without changing calibration approval. No utterance state carries between captures.
- **AU-010:** Every exit path releases buffers and deletes any decoder temporary file in a guaranteed cleanup block.

## 9. Deterministic normalization

Normalization creates one auxiliary view and never overwrites the original observation.

- **NORM-001:** Apply DC removal and peak-safe loudness normalization with no clipping.
- **NORM-002:** Estimate speaking rate only from local VAD regions; time-stretch is bounded to `[0.85, 1.18]` and preserves pitch.
- **NORM-003:** Optional spectral denoise must be deterministic and disabled when noise estimation is unreliable.
- **NORM-004:** Formant/VTLN warping is bounded to `[0.88, 1.12]`; selection is based on a versioned development rule, not inferred age or diagnosis.
- **NORM-005:** No generative model, identity cloning, adult speaker injection, or hallucinated content is permitted.
- **NORM-006:** Record transformation names and parameters with the attempt metrics, but never store transformed audio.
- **NORM-007:** If normalization fails, continue with original-only evidence and apply the calibrator’s missing-view policy; do not synthesize evidence.

### 10.1 Mobile review student (DEC-028)

- **MST-001:** The student accepts exactly 32,000 mono float32 PCM samples at 16 kHz. Android converts signed PCM16 to `[-1, 1]`, deterministically centre-crops longer speech and zero-pads shorter speech. No transcript, cloud service, or teacher runtime is needed on device.
- **MST-002:** The review vocabulary is the canonical Android 19-word starter set plus `OTHER`, with immutable ordered labels and Tamil display metadata. Unknown or missing target IDs yield `DEV_UNAVAILABLE` and an official `UNSCORABLE` result.
- **MST-003:** Training uses speaker-disjoint train, calibration and evaluation partitions. Adult/public evaluation is engineering evidence only; child recordings and the reserved child-validation folder are excluded from training, calibration and threshold selection.
- **MST-004:** The student emits a 64-dimensional embedding and 20 logits. Training combines supervised cross-entropy with soft teacher targets derived from the frozen IndicConformer engineering classifier; teacher provenance and artifact hashes are recorded.
- **MST-005:** Export produces a checked FP32 ONNX model followed by static QDQ S8S8 INT8 quantization using only the calibration partition. Integration reports raw top-1 agreement but gates the policy-reachable region: where both models have top-1 margin at least 0.10, FP32/INT8 top-1 agreement is at least 99%. Whole-evaluation probability mean absolute error is at most 0.02 and adult-evaluation macro-F1 degradation is at most one percentage point. Lower-margin disagreement is unavailable/uncertain and can never produce `DEV_MATCH`.
- **MST-006:** The installed bundle manifest binds every shipped file by SHA-256 and records model, dataset, vocabulary, preprocessing, calibration, reference and code versions. Missing, changed or unsupported files yield `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`; there is no server fallback.
- **MST-007:** Adult-engineering demonstration bands are calibrated from the frozen adult calibration split and yield only `DEV_MATCH`, `DEV_TRY_AGAIN`, `DEV_UNCERTAIN`, or `DEV_UNAVAILABLE`. They never populate `word_match_probability`, child progress correctness, PASS or RETRY.
- **MST-008:** Android runs inference outside the UI thread through ONNX Runtime Mobile. Break, Stop, screen disposal and a new capture invalidate stale work; buffers are cleared after use. CPU is mandatory; NNAPI is opt-in only after model-usability, numerical-parity, latency and device-stability checks.
- **MST-009:** The debug/review APK visibly labels engineering evidence and continues to record only capture reason/event counts. Release builds keep the review scorer disabled until a later SSOT revision binds a child-separated approved mobile calibration and the complete active vocabulary.
- **MST-010:** Verification records model sizes and hashes, quantization parity, held-out adult metrics, repeated emulator/device latency, APK install/start, offline microphone flow, cancellation, and absence of network dependence. Emulator evidence is not physical-device, thermal, accessibility, child or clinical evidence.
- **MST-011:** The v1 ONNX graph performs deterministic per-utterance DC removal and RMS normalization with an explicit numerical floor and bounded amplitude before feature extraction. Training, export, desktop inference and Android invoke that same graph; no client-side approximation or persisted normalized audio is permitted.
- **MST-012:** The v1 encoder preserves coarse left-to-right word structure through fixed temporal pooling before the 64-dimensional embedding and 20 logits. Global pooling that discards all word order is not an acceptable v1 implementation.
- **MST-013:** Training-only augmentation deterministically samples bounded gain, time shift, 0.75–2.25 speaking-rate/room-envelope scaling, additive noise, low-pass coloration and room echo/reverberation up to 400 ms. Calibration and evaluation audio are never augmented. Every augmentation range and random seed is recorded with the artifact; captured user audio is excluded.
- **MST-014:** A v1 candidate must satisfy MST-005, achieve adult INT8 macro-F1 of at least 0.30 without degrading the frozen v0 adult evaluation result, and classify the known held-out adult `Amma` fixture as its target both directly and after speaker-to-phone recapture on the named physical ARM64 review device. The acoustic canary is a device-path smoke test, not a general accuracy, child-readiness or clinical claim, and it may not be satisfied by threshold changes.
- **MST-015:** The promoted v1 manifest, calibration, centroids, labels and ONNX model are rebuilt and SHA-256-bound together. Android fails closed on a v0/v1 mixture, missing asset or integrity mismatch. Promotion evidence records the APK hash, installed-device hash, package version, physical device identifier, capture/VAD/model diagnostics and offline execution result.
- **MST-016:** After VAD acceptance and before MST-001 padding, Android deterministically removes only low-energy room tail outside the active word envelope using fixed 20 ms frames, an eight-percent peak-frame RMS threshold with an absolute floor, and 60 ms boundary margins. Empty or sub-minimum output remains unavailable. This transient operation records lengths only, never PCM, and must be unit-tested against leading/trailing noise and weak valid boundaries.
- **MST-017:** Each target in the v1 calibration records the median sample length of its frozen adult/public training references. After MST-016, Android linearly resamples the transient active word to that target-bound duration before MST-001 centre-padding. Missing/out-of-range duration metadata makes the bundle unavailable. This deterministic rate normalization is label-conditioned preprocessing, never evidence of correctness, and its version and calibration hash are bound into the bundle.

## 11. Word classifier and reference DTW

### 11.1 Closed-vocabulary classifier

- **CLS-001:** The classifier vocabulary is exactly the versioned therapist-approved word set plus `OTHER`.
- **CLS-002:** Input is masked mean plus attentive/statistical pooling over encoder frames; training groups all recordings from a child into one split.
- **CLS-003:** Output is calibrated class probability, entropy, top-k alternatives, vocabulary version, classifier version.
- **CLS-004:** A class absent from the installed artifact is unscorable, not an inferred nearest label.
- **CLS-005:** `OTHER` and acoustically confusable Tamil words are included in training/evaluation to measure false acceptance.

### 11.2 Embedding DTW

- **DTW-001:** Reference audio is therapist-approved, locally packaged, and represented by model embeddings generated with the identical model/feature versions.
- **DTW-002:** Store embeddings only in the signed application reference bank; they are adult/reference assets, never child-derived session data.
- **DTW-003:** Use cosine frame distance and a Sakoe-Chiba band of 20% of the longer sequence.
- **DTW-004:** Normalize path cost by path length and convert similarity through the calibration artifact; a hand-written distance threshold is forbidden in clinical mode.
- **DTW-005:** An active release word requires 32–64 accepted adult references from at least eight speakers, with no more than four accepted references per speaker. Runtime similarity uses the calibrated aggregate of the best three distinct-speaker matches, not the single minimum distance, and records the reference-bank version.
- **DTW-006:** Empty, dimension-mismatched, non-finite, or version-mismatched sequences are unavailable evidence.
- **DTW-007:** Reference selection rejects duplicates, train/evaluation leakage, invalid/no-speech captures, clipping, excessive noise, unknown provenance, and unknown or incompatible redistribution terms. A word below coverage remains inactive and unscorable.

## 12. Evidence fusion and decision semantics

### 12.1 Feature vector

The calibrator receives, with explicit missing masks:

`p_raw_target`, `p_normalized_target`, `raw_normalized_disagreement`, `p_classifier_target`, `classifier_entropy`, `dtw_similarity`, `raw_top1_is_target`, `normalized_top1_is_target`, `duration_quality`, and `noise_quality`.

The fitted model is logistic regression or isotonic calibration selected only on a child-separated calibration set. Its artifact contains schema version, coefficients/model payload, feature order, missing-value rules, thresholds, model hash, classifier hash, reference-bank hash, vocabulary hash, dataset manifest hash, creation time, and approval status.

### 12.2 Hard gates

- **FUS-001:** Invalid capture, missing primary IndicConformer, integrity failure, timeout, or unapproved calibrator yields `UNSCORABLE`.
- **FUS-002:** PASS requires adequate quality and agreement from at least two independent evidence families: token, classifier, and DTW.
- **FUS-003:** DTW alone, normalized-view evidence alone, browser transcript, or generative voice conversion can never produce PASS.
- **FUS-004:** If raw and normalized views materially disagree, PASS is prohibited unless the approved artifact explicitly calibrates the disagreement region with sufficient validation support.
- **FUS-005:** A clear wrong/confusable word with adequate evidence yields `RETRY`, using neutral child-facing language.
- **FUS-006:** The uncertainty interval between retry and pass thresholds yields `UNSCORABLE`, not a forced binary result.
- **FUS-007:** Prototype mode without target-child calibration must set `scoring_ready=false`; tests may use a clearly labelled synthetic fixture artifact.

### 12.3 Decision fields

- `PASS`: sufficient calibrated evidence that the attempted word matches the known target under the frozen operating point.
- `RETRY`: sufficient evidence of mismatch or incomplete target; this is not a clinical diagnosis or statement of inability.
- `UNSCORABLE`: the system cannot safely judge this attempt because of quality, uncertainty, missing capability, or policy.

The numeric field is `word_match_probability`, not “pronunciation accuracy.” It is nullable. The compatibility `accuracy` field, while old clients remain, equals probability times 100 and must be labelled “word match evidence” in UI; it is null for unscorable attempts.

## 14. Session and adaptive policy

### 14.1 Deterministic policy

Before learning is enabled, rules map assessment and state to approved actions.

| Condition | Response intent | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| PASS | CELEBRATE | next approved word or session close |
| RETRY, attempts remain | ENCOURAGE_RETRY | same word with one approved support |
| UNSCORABLE due capture | NEUTRAL_RETRY | recapture without correctness judgement |
| UNSCORABLE due capability | CAREGIVER_TECHNICAL_HELP | pause scoring; do not pretend success |
| max retries | OFFER_BREAK or SWITCH_APPROVED | break/easier approved word |
| child/caregiver stop | CALM_END | stop immediately |
| session fatigue limit | OFFER_BREAK | break; no policy override |

### 14.2 Contextual bandit

The following specifications are reserved for offline research. DEC-011 disables active/shadow learning in V1 runtime sessions.

- **RL-001:** Context may include recent PASS/RETRY/UNSCORABLE sequence, attempts, word mastery, support level, elapsed session time, breaks, configured sensory preferences, clinician goal tags, and prior response to prompt modalities.
- **RL-002:** It must not include direct identity, raw audio, embeddings, diagnosis severity, inferred emotion, or protected/sensitive proxy features.
- **RL-003:** Actions are limited to repeat, related/easier approved word, picture cue, approved adult example, approved prompt style, approved reward animation, break, or safe end.
- **RL-004:** Reward components are successful word, improvement after support, voluntary continuation, and return after a break. Penalties are repeated failure, stop request, abandonment, excessive prompting, and rejected action. Time seated alone has zero reward.
- **RL-005:** Hard constraints filter actions before ranking; the policy never sees forbidden actions.
- **RL-006:** LinUCB uses per-action ridge-regression matrices, regularization `lambda=1.0`, initial exploration `alpha=0.25`, and versioned deterministic feature scaling. Parameters are configuration, not medical constants.
- **RL-007:** Every decision logs context schema version, eligible actions, selected action, propensity/score, mode, policy version, outcome, reward components, and override reason.
- **RL-008:** Modes are `deterministic`, `shadow`, and `active`. Default is deterministic. Shadow recommendations do not affect the child.
- **RL-009:** Active mode requires offline replay evaluation, shadow safety review, therapist approval, guardian consent where required, and a rollback switch.
- **RL-010:** DQN, PPO, SAC, RLHF, and unrestricted online RL are prohibited in V1. Conservative Q-Learning may be researched later only after sufficient longitudinal logged data and a revised SSOT.

### 14.3 Complete session lifecycle

- **SES-001:** One active/paused word session per child; assigned plan/version and current therapist relationship are rechecked for reads, changes, and attempt completion. New word plans include explicit limits. Legacy plans without limits cannot silently acquire unlimited sessions.
- **SES-002:** Limits include retries per word (0–3), session seconds (60–900), total captures (1–60), and break seconds (15–300). The engineering default is 2 retries, 600 seconds, 40 captures, and a 30-second break. These are conservative software bounds, not a clinical prescription; therapist approval remains a release gate. Elapsed session time is measured from server creation, including pauses; resume never resets it.
- **SES-003:** Pause/stop invalidates an in-flight claim immediately. A late completion cannot become PASS. A timed-out/crashed inference lease can be recovered without accepting its later result. A bounded single-flight model executor retains occupancy until the actual worker finishes, even when the request times out.
- **SES-004:** Resume/switch after retry-limit or capability pause requires the assigned therapist or administrator; the existing `user` child-view identity cannot authorize that adult continuation. Resume after a retry limit requires a completed break and switching to another assigned word, without resetting total captures or elapsed time. A voluntary child break may resume on the same word after the configured break interval without adult intervention, if the plan is still current and no other safety limit has fired; it never resets attempts or elapsed time. Session fatigue/total-capture exhaustion ends the session. Starting after a recently ended/exhausted session respects the previous plan's break interval.
- **SES-005:** Persist only bounded pseudonymous attempt summaries and session state, atomically with the transition. A duplicate attempt cannot run inference twice or advance twice. Summary output contains decision counts and versions, with no transcript, candidates, audio, or embeddings. Database failure yields an explicit unavailable result; no success is acknowledged before the transition is persisted.
- **SES-006:** The operator lab has a separate diagnostic identity, endpoint, and evidence label. Creating or mutating a real child plan from the diagnostic flow is prohibited. Diagnostic audio/results remain ephemeral; no production scoring approval is inferred from successful pipeline execution.
- **SES-007:** A voluntary Break / Stop control aborts microphone capture and in-flight inference immediately. The first touch pauses server state; choosing End stops it. If the pause request cannot be confirmed, the client stays non-listening and shows an explicit unavailable state; it cannot silently resume.
- **SES-008:** Parent-provided comfort, interests, preferred prompt style, and estimated comfortable block length initialize a bounded session schedule. A missing questionnaire uses conservative therapist-plan defaults, not fabricated observations. Planned breaks are offered at block boundaries; the child may break or stop sooner and is never forced to remain seated.

- **RPT-005:** Bind a therapist approval to canonical report SHA-256, canonical remarks/plan SHA-256, signer account and credential ID, server challenge, verification result, timestamp, and schema/artifact versions. Require a registered asymmetric signing credential with user verification (for example a correctly verified passkey assertion); neither a client-supplied `approved` flag, typed name, password hash, shared HMAC, nor a bare digest is an individual therapist signature.
- **RPT-006:** A review is append-only. Any changed report, remark, plan, or signer requires a new version and signature. Exports carry enough non-secret verification material to recheck integrity and signer provenance, but never private keys or child recordings. Signature validity proves origin and alteration resistance, not clinical correctness.
- **RPT-007:** Report and review endpoints enforce current therapist-child relationship, purpose/consent, strict schemas, idempotency, rate limits, and audit events. Missing identity, credential, assignment, consent, database, or signature verification fails closed.

## 17. Data, training, and calibration governance

### 17.0 Human-evidence workflow

- **GOV-001:** Child recording consent and any applicable research ethics approval are external human acts. The application may register a scoped, hashed evidence receipt from the account caregiver and an independent administrator verification or reason-coded rejection; absent verification remains pending. The caregiver can withdraw the receipt, and withdrawal immediately supersedes a previous verification. A receipt never retroactively authorizes an earlier child recording. Raw consent documents, identity proofs, or child audio are not stored in the product database or repository.
- **GOV-002:** An administrator may register a therapist/reviewer qualification only after an external credential check, recording the scope, credential evidence hash, verifier, and time. Self-verification is prohibited. A self-registered therapist role alone is not qualification for adult-reference, canary, or clinical approval.
- **GOV-003:** Every adult-reference approval identifies the exact source-manifest hash, sample ID, canonical word ID, audio hash, reviewer, human provenance, capture-valid decision, and bounded quality score. The exported approval manifest must be immutable/hash-bound and authenticated by a locally held 32-byte-or-longer review signing key. The builder rejects missing/invalid signatures, missing global human approval, duplicate or changed samples, unqualified provenance, capture-invalid rows, or mismatched audio/source metadata. Audio playback for review stays in a local authorized workstation workflow, never a child session.
- **GOV-004:** Canary candidates are distinct from approved release canaries. A positive/confusable/invalid suite requires complete category/active-word coverage, source non-overlap, audio integrity, a qualified human expected-label review authenticated by the same local signing key, and passing technical execution against the finalized suite hash. The finalized suite itself carries an authenticated exact-content signature; a `human_approved` boolean without a valid signed review and suite is never release evidence. A technical pass alone is not approval. An engineering bundle may stage the exact suite before its first technical run, but production requires that run.
- **GOV-005:** Clinical-content review covers ten independently hashed sections: vocabulary, Tamil prompts, voice assets, avatar states, result wording, action set, difficulty, retry/break bounds, test protocol, and limitations. Only a separately credential-verified assigned therapist may approve an exact immutable content bundle; changed hashes or revoked qualification return it to pending. Release gate status remains false while child validation or any other mandatory gate is pending.
- **GOV-006:** Human evidence records are append-only with authenticated actor, timestamp, evidence hash, decision, and audit event. Client-supplied reviewer IDs or booleans are not approval authority. Missing database, identity, relationship, hash, or evidence fails closed; exports contain metadata only, not raw audio, signatures, or private identity documents.
- **GOV-007:** Caregiver/therapist UI must show genuine pending/approved/rejected state and real source data, never fictional therapist ratings, appointments, clinical notes, or generated human sign-offs. Operator-facing draft suggestions are labelled as such.

- **DATA-001:** Public/adult Tamil data can prove pipeline operation but cannot establish target-user validity.
- **DATA-002:** Target validation uses ethics-approved, guardian-consented, age-appropriate recordings and therapist labels.
- **DATA-003:** Splits are by child. Repeated sessions, near duplicates, siblings/household leakage where relevant, prompts, and derived augmentations remain within one split.
- **DATA-004:** Final test children are excluded from model training, classifier training, threshold selection, normalization tuning, and reference selection.
- **DATA-005:** Labels distinguish intended word, other word, partial/no meaningful word, capture invalid, and adjudication uncertainty.
- **DATA-006:** At least two qualified raters label validation data; disagreements are adjudicated and inter-rater agreement is reported.
- **DATA-007:** Augmentation provenance records source, transformation, seed, parent sample, and split. Synthetic results are reported separately.
- **DATA-008:** Model, classifier, reference bank, calibration, vocabulary, dataset manifest, and code commit are immutable and cross-hashed for a release.
- **DATA-013:** Calibration carries the executable live-pipeline fingerprint, including decoder, normalization, VAD, classifier/DTW transforms and feature construction. Production verification and the assessment service reject absent/mismatched fingerprints; a provenance sidecar alone cannot authorize scoring. Old artifacts require regeneration, not an approval toggle. Synthetic test fixtures remain explicitly isolated.
- **DATA-009:** Raw child audio retention for research, if approved, is outside the product database in controlled research storage with independent consent and deletion policy.
- **DATA-010:** The V1 engineering corpus targets 100 active Tamil words, 32–64 accepted adult references per active word, and at least 10,000 `OTHER`/confusable adult examples. Counts are coverage targets, not permission to lower quality or provenance requirements.
- **DATA-011:** Corpus manifests record source dataset, source revision, original identifier, speaker pseudonym, canonical word ID, licence/provenance evidence, checksum, duration, quality decisions, deduplication group, and split. Raw public/adult audio is a build input and is not shipped in the runtime reference bundle; only approved embeddings are packaged.
- **DATA-012:** Vocabulary activation is data-dependent. The 100-word registry is the intended V1 curriculum, but only words meeting reference, classifier, held-out evaluation, and human-review gates enter an installed active bundle; unsupported words fail closed without shrinking the registry silently.

## 18. Evaluation and release gates

### 18.0 Fortnightly therapist checkup

- **CHK-001:** For a child assigned to a therapist, compute a checkup due timestamp exactly 14 days after the assignment timestamp or the most recent therapist-confirmed completed checkup, whichever is later. Existing assignments without a known timestamp show `schedule_unestablished`, not a fabricated due date; unassigned children show `therapist_unassigned`.
- **CHK-002:** A caregiver may request a checkup only with the currently assigned therapist; a pending, confirmed, cancelled, or no-show appointment does not reset the due clock. Only that therapist can confirm or complete it, and completion cannot precede the scheduled start. Admin may correct a record with a separately audited reason, not silently impersonate the therapist.
- **CHK-003:** The caregiver sees due/scheduled/overdue status and a booking action. An overdue checkup is a reminder, not a word-practice lock, forced continuation, or claim that a medical visit occurred. No appointment is auto-created; no audio-derived data enters notifications.
- **CHK-004:** Checkup booking, rescheduling, status changes, and cancellation are atomic conditional transitions with role/relationship checks and audit events. Conflicting active slots and duplicate open checkup requests are rejected. Legacy appointments have no checkup authority until explicitly created with checkup kind and therapist confirmation.

### 18.1 Primary metrics

- Wrong-word false PASS rate overall must be at most 2.0% with a reported 95% confidence interval.
- No tested word or predeclared subgroup may exceed 5.0% false PASS without blocking or disabling that slice.
- Report PASS precision/recall, RETRY precision/recall, UNSCORABLE rate, risk-coverage curve, expected calibration error, Brier score, and confusion by word.
- Report results by age band 7–10 and 11–14, child, sex where ethically appropriate, microphone/device, quiet/noisy condition, pause/repetition case, and word difficulty, subject to minimum sample/reporting safeguards.
- Speech-end-to-result latency target is median at most 1.0 s and p95 below 1.5 s on the selected GPU workstation; avatar-visible reaction adds at most 300 ms.

### 18.2 Mandatory challenge cases

Correct target; confusable wrong word; unrelated word; long within-word pause; repeated beginning; partial word; background adult speech; avatar prompt leakage; whisper; soft voice; clipping; noise; no speech; unsupported codec; overly long input; child/caregiver cancellation; model timeout; artifact mismatch; original/normalized disagreement.

### 18.3 Release gates

- **GATE-01 Engineering:** contracts, DTW, normalization, calibrator gates, policy, privacy allow-list, and API tests pass using labelled synthetic/fake model outputs.
- **GATE-02 Model:** real IndicConformer loads offline, produces Tamil candidates and embeddings, and passes integrity/latency tests.
- **GATE-03 Calibration:** child-separated therapist-labelled calibration artifact is approved and false-PASS operating point passes.
- **GATE-04 Integration:** owned React frontend, Android microphone, and Unity avatar complete physical-device sessions on a private network and in offline inference mode.
- **GATE-05 Privacy/security:** packet/filesystem/log/recovery evidence passes; no forbidden outbound or persistent voice data is observed.
- **GATE-06 Clinical/research:** clinician approves vocabulary, prompt assets, result wording, test protocol, and limitations.

Before GATE-03 the product is labelled **technical prototype — scoring not clinically validated**. Before every gate passes, it is not described as production-ready.

## 19. Testing strategy

- Unit tests cover audio validation, Unicode normalization, deterministic normalization bounds, cosine DTW, feature schema, artifact mismatch, hard fusion gates, response mapping, LinUCB math, action constraints, and recursive sync rejection.
- Contract tests validate canonical, legacy, Unity, storage, and sync schemas.
- Integration tests inject a fake acoustic model and real service graph; fake outputs are marked synthetic and never reported as model performance.
- Model tests require installed real artifacts and use a separate marker/job so core CI cannot imply model readiness.
- Frontend tests verify PASS/RETRY/UNSCORABLE rendering, null evidence, stop/pause, and no numeric child-facing accuracy.
- Android/Unity tests cover microphone lifecycle, prompt/listen exclusion, cancellation, private-LAN authentication, and degraded connectivity.
- Security tests cover authorization, path traversal, upload bombs, MIME spoofing, malformed media, replay/idempotency, secret scanning, dependency scanning, artifact signature/hash checks, and redacted errors.

Every completion claim names the exact command, exit code, date, environment, and whether evidence was synthetic, local, physical-device, or target-user clinical.

## 20. Deployment

### 20.1 V1 topology

- End-user Android sessions execute VAD, the compact student and its matching artifacts, deterministic policy, local records, approved prompts, and avatar on the device; no FastAPI/cloud service is required during a session.
- The Windows/Linux FastAPI backend remains the reference implementation and local engineering/test harness. It may use the installed teacher on CPU or a development GPU. It binds to loopback by default; private-LAN tests require explicit authenticated configuration.
- Firewall denies public ingress. CORS and trusted hosts are explicit. The inference service is never deployed to a public Render/Vercel endpoint.
- Models and approved assets are installed before the session. The child flow performs no model or media download.
- Production configuration disables debug mode, fake adapters, test calibrators, energy-only PASS, face analysis, cloud speech providers, and unrestricted analytics.

### 20.2 Configuration

Required environment/config values include deployment mode, model path/hash, classifier path/hash, reference-bank path/hash, calibration path/hash, vocabulary version, policy path/version, database URL, encryption-key reference, allowed hosts/origins, maximum upload bytes, inference timeout, VAD pause tolerance, sync enabled flag, therapist-service allow-list, and structured-log level.

Secrets are supplied through the OS/container secret mechanism and never committed. Configuration startup validation fails closed when production scoring artifacts are incomplete.

### 20.3 Operations

- Liveness never loads the model.
- Readiness verifies application dependencies.
- Scoring readiness verifies all artifact compatibility and one local canary inference.
- Warm-up is bounded and observable; startup remains responsive.
- Metrics include counts/latency by decision and reason code, model readiness, timeouts, and policy overrides, without speech content.
- A prior signed artifact bundle is retained for rollback.
- Backups contain encrypted structured metrics only.

## 22. Definition of Done

The application is complete only when all six release gates pass and evidence is committed. Specifically:

- The owned frontend runs against the canonical word-level backend without unsafe legacy scoring.
- A real target word attempt produces PASS, RETRY, or UNSCORABLE from the approved local artifact bundle.
- A missing/broken model, invalid audio, uncertainty, or artifact mismatch never produces PASS.
- The deterministic policy and any bandit action remain within therapist constraints and honor stop/break immediately.
- No voice-derived data is stored or synchronized in product operation.
- A complete session works without internet and meets target latency on selected hardware.
- The wrong-word false PASS and subgroup gates pass on the untouched therapist-labelled child test set.
- React, Android, Unity, backend, model, privacy, recovery, and physical-device evidence is reproducible.
- Documentation and UI identify the system as assistive, word-level, non-diagnostic, and not clinically validated until evidence supports the claim.

## 23. What breaks first

1. **Representative labelled data:** without it, calibration and target-user performance claims cannot be completed. Mitigation: fail closed and prioritize the ethics/annotation protocol.
2. **IndicConformer deployment size/latency:** the teacher may be too slow or memory-heavy. Mitigation: benchmark early, then export/quantize/distil only against frozen parity gates.
3. **Wrong-word false acceptance:** ASR may overfit a known target. Mitigation: confusable/OTHER classifier data, multi-family PASS rule, abstention, predeclared false-PASS gate.
4. **Child variability and pauses:** VAD/normalization may remove useful speech. Mitigation: keep original view authoritative, bounded transformations, pause challenge set, normalization ablations.
5. **Frontend legacy semantics:** numeric “accuracy” and browser transcripts may mislead. Mitigation: one canonical contract, compatibility adapter, safety-copy tests, remove legacy influence.
6. **Privacy regressions:** broad existing platform modules or face/cloud dependencies may transmit sensitive data. Mitigation: isolate the word path, disable face inference, outbound allow-list, packet and log evidence.
7. **Bandit reward harm:** optimizing engagement can pressure a child. Mitigation: zero reward for time seated, hard stop/break priority, therapist action set, shadow stage, rollback.

## 24. Alternatives rejected

- Phoneme CTC + forced alignment + GOP: rejected for this approved V1 scope because the target is word-level and a trustworthy Tamil child phoneme head/labels are not currently established.
- Waveform DTW to an adult reference: rejected as primary evidence due to speaker/pitch/rate sensitivity.
- Child-to-adult generative conversion before scoring: rejected because it can alter linguistic content and hide errors.
- Browser speech recognition: rejected because it is platform/cloud-dependent and not authoritative Tamil word evidence.
- English Wav2Vec2 with romanized Tamil: rejected because token space and training language do not support the claim.
- Facial emotion/stress inference: rejected because few landmarks do not establish a clinically meaningful state.
- End-to-end deep RL at launch: rejected due to sample inefficiency, unsafe exploration, weak reward definition, and poor auditability.
- Public cloud inference: rejected due to child voice privacy, reliability, and offline goals.
- Embedding the full teacher model in Android V1: deferred until export and parity benchmarks prove feasibility.
